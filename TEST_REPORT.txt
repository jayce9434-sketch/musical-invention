PROGRESSION DESIGN
The Grand Run completion path now depends on multiple systems rather than only purchasing upgrades: map contracts, maps, bugs, knockers, bug Mastery, Souvenirs, run challenges, mutations, Gadgets, and permanent upgrades. Economy costs and late-map multipliers are intended to place a normal first full journey around the multi-hour range rather than a short session. Exact playtime varies by skill and route choices.
